## Clean-before-export vs clean-in-import

**Clean in AppSheet before export** (human judgment, can't be mechanical):

- Q2 the 1 multi-patient txn with a 1997-char `t_PatientRef`
- Q8 the 665 admin-no-txn billing policy
- Q4 the 5 duplicate charges (canonical record per pair)

**Clean in the importer** (mechanical, idempotent, testable): everything else —
ID normalisation, phone formatting, status mapping, date clamping, dedup
detection, catalog collapse, discount mapping, transaction consolidation.

Recommended triage threshold: only clean in-source where ≥10 rows are affected
AND it's clinically/financially meaningful. Everything else rides in with a
warning tag surfaced in v2's normal UI.
