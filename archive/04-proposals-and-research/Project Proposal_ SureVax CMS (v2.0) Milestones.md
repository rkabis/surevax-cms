### **Project Costing & Milestones**

The total investment of **₱120,000.00** is broken down into five distinct phases. Each payment is tied to the completion of specific **full-stack deliverables** (Backend Logic \+ Frontend UI), ensuring the company pays for tangible, usable software features at every step.

| Phase             | Cost (PHP)   | Focus Area                           | Broad Deliverables (Backend \+ Frontend UI)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** _(1.5 wks)_ | **₱40,000**  | **Setup, Planning & Infrastructure** | **Consultation & Architecture Foundation** • **Stakeholder Consultation:** Sessions with current users to gather feedback for UI/UX improvements, which will be integrated into the designs for subsequent phases. _Note: Any additional functionality requests outside the core scope (in spirit) will be documented for future releases._ • **Data Isolation Architecture:** Configuration of strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure data is completely isolated between branches. • **Server Provisioning:** Setup of Virtual Private Server (VPS) and Managed PostgreSQL Database. • **Services Schema Design:** Abstracting the database to handle a wide range of "Services" (not just vaccines) for future scalability.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **2** _(2 wks)_   | **₱30,000**  | **Operational MVP (Core CRUD)**      | **Essential Clinic Operations & Interfaces** • **Single Service Intake:** A generalized UI module to log individual services. Examples include: _Anti-Rabies Vaccine, Anti-Tetanus Shot, ERIG, and Medical Certificate issuance_. • **Transaction UI:** A flexible Point-of-Sale interface allowing for: \- Assigning transactions to specific payment methods (Cash, GCash, PayMongo, Receivables, etc). \- Tracking "Receivables" (unpaid balances). \- Handling **split payments** across different accounts (e.g., Part Cash / Part Digital) for a single transaction. • **Patient Management UI:** Responsive screens for searching and updating patient profiles and pet status. • **Staff Portal:** Secure login and session management.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **3** _(2 wks)_   | **₱25,000**  | **SureVax Algorithms & Functions**   | **Smart Logic & Specialized UI** • **Service Bundle Engine:** Frontend forms and backend logic that allow a single "Macro" entry to auto-generate multiple distinct service records. _Example: A common 'Day 0' bundle of ARV \+ Anti-Tetanus \+ ERIG._ • **Automated Standing Orders:** Algorithms that automatically generate "Pending" future service rows based on protocol intervals (e.g., Rabies Day 3/7/14), displayed in a dedicated "Future Orders" view. • **Dynamic Scheduling:** Logic to recalculate service due dates based on "Actual" vs. "Ideal" administration times. • **Patient Reminders:** Button to automatically open default SMS app to text patient reminders for vaccine appointments • **Pet Reminders:** Functionality to track Pet status and adjust standing orders based on status • **Acknowledgement Receipt Generation:** Generate acknowledgement receipts on transaction posting or on demand |
| **4** _(1 wk)_    | **₱15,000**  | **Reporting & Dashboards**           | **Management Visibility (Visual Interfaces)** • **Visual Analytics Dashboard:** A React-based command center replicating Looker Studio metrics (Revenue, Volume) directly within the app. • **Branch Reporting UI:** One-click generation of "End of Day" summaries for collections and service counts. • **Document Generation:** Auto-formatted PDF outputs for Patient Health Cards, ready for printing directly from the system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **5** _(1 wk)_    | **₱10,000**  | **Migration & Go-Live**              | **Transition & Deployment** • **Legacy Data Migration:** Extraction, cleaning, and import of historical data from Google Sheets to the new SQL Services structure. • **UAT (User Acceptance Testing):** Final stress testing of all UI workflows and backend calculations. • **Production Release:** Domain DNS setup and official system handover.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Total**         | **₱120,000** |                                      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

#### **Payment Terms:**

- **Invoicing:** Invoices will be issued upon the formal sign-off/approval of each phase's deliverables except for the 1st phase which will be paid out ASAP.
- **Maintenance:** As per the developer retainer agreement, ongoing maintenance and bug fixes are included at no additional cost. Additional functionality beyond this initial scope subject to new project costing processes.

**_Initial scope, in spirit, includes everything that the current CMS is functionally capable of but redeveloped in a new platform._** **_If a CMS v1 functionality is available but not explicitly included in the scope above, it will be added onto the project scope at no additional cost._**
